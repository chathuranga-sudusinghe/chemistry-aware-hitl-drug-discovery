# Candidate Research Questions

## Status

**PROPOSED — pre-protocol.** These questions will be revised after target selection and deeper literature review.

### RQ1 — Main question

Under a fixed medicinal-chemist review budget, can a chemistry-aware expert-review utility policy improve multi-objective lead-selection decisions compared with uncertainty-based, information-gain-based, score-based, and random review-prioritization strategies?

### RQ2 — Chemistry signal value

Which combination of chemistry and model signals best predicts when medicinal-chemist intervention materially changes a lead-selection decision?

Candidate signals include uncertainty, model disagreement, applicability-domain distance, scaffold novelty, SAR inconsistency, structural liabilities, synthetic feasibility, ADMET conflicts, and target-specific interaction evidence.

### RQ3 — Escalation versus prioritization

Does separating expert escalation from review prioritization improve review efficiency relative to a single acquisition/ranking score?

### RQ4 — Review-capacity sensitivity

How does the proposed policy behave as expert-review capacity is reduced across predefined budgets?

### RQ5 — Structured human feedback

Can structured medicinal-chemistry feedback improve future escalation/prioritization decisions without overfitting to individual reviewer preferences or noisy feedback?

### RQ6 — Reliability under distribution shift

Does chemistry-aware expert escalation improve decision reliability for candidates that are outside or near the boundary of the predictive model's applicability domain?

## Notes

The final RQs must be linked to a selected chemistry task, defined endpoints, a feasible ground-truth strategy, and a pre-specified statistical analysis plan.
