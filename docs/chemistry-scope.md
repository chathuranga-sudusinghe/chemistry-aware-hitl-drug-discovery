# Chemistry Scope

## Status

**OPEN / PROPOSED.** The exact therapeutic target and lead-optimization task are not yet selected.

## Preferred domain

The current preferred domain is small-molecule drug discovery with emphasis on **lead optimization**, because it naturally supports deep chemistry questions involving SAR, multi-objective trade-offs, developability, synthesis, and target-specific evidence.

## Chemistry dimensions to preserve

The final task should allow substantive analysis of several of the following:

### Molecular structure

- functional groups and electronic/steric context;
- stereochemistry;
- protonation and ionization;
- tautomerism;
- conformational flexibility;
- reactive or unstable motifs;
- scaffold identity and novelty.

### Medicinal chemistry

- potency and selectivity;
- Structure-Activity Relationship (SAR);
- drug-likeness only where scientifically relevant;
- lipophilicity, solubility, permeability, and related physicochemical properties;
- metabolic liabilities;
- toxicity and safety signals;
- multiparameter trade-offs.

### Developability and synthesis

- synthetic accessibility versus genuine route feasibility;
- known problematic substructures;
- competing objectives between potency, exposure, safety, and synthesis.

### Protein-ligand context

If a structure-enabled target is selected and the structural evidence is reliable, the project may consider:

- binding-site compatibility;
- key residue interactions;
- steric clashes;
- hydrogen-bond and electrostatic plausibility;
- hydrophobic contacts;
- ligand strain and pose plausibility.

Docking score alone must not be treated as experimental binding affinity.

## Target-selection criteria

A candidate protein/therapeutic system should be evaluated against:

- public data availability and licensing;
- number and quality of measured activity records;
- SAR richness and analogue series;
- structural data availability;
- assay comparability and endpoint clarity;
- availability of useful ADMET/developability evidence;
- publication saturation versus a meaningful gap;
- computational feasibility;
- ability to create a defensible held-out evaluation.

## Not yet accepted

No specific disease, protein, dataset, assay, docking method, molecular generator, or ADMET panel is currently accepted.
