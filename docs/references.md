## Reference policy

Before a new `REF-xxx` entry is marked VERIFIED:

1. confirm the title, authorship, year, venue, and DOI/authoritative URL;
2. read enough of the source to support the exact claim being made;
3. record limitations relevant to the project;
4. do not infer novelty merely because a specific phrase is absent from the title/abstract.
